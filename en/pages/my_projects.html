EpicVault

An application designed to manage tabletop role-playing campaigns. It allows users to organize rooms, notes, and characters, manage public and private inventories, and automate money-related calculations.

The project includes two formats: a web application built with Angular, Express, and MongoDB, and an Android version.

Technologies: Angular, Express, and MongoDB.
