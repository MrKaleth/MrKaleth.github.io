A SPACE FOR EVERY ADVENTURE

EpicVault.

Role-playing campaign management / your adventures, organized

EpicVault is a project designed to simplify role-playing campaign management. It brings together notes, characters, and inventories for each campaign in one place. It also features automated money management and separates public resources from private ones to better organize adventures and each participant's information.

WHAT YOU CAN ORGANIZE

Features.

01

Friends system

Connect with other participants and make organizing your gaming sessions easier.

02

Shared rooms

Create spaces to gather and organize information for each campaign.

03

Campaign notes

Save notes and important information about your adventures.

04

Character creation

Create and organize the characters taking part in your role-playing campaigns.

05

Automated money management

Automate money-related calculations to simplify management and reduce manual calculations during gaming sessions.

06

Public inventory

Maintain a shared inventory whose items are displayed in a public area of the campaign, allowing participants to view shared resources.

07

Private inventory

Manage personal items and resources in a separate inventory. Its contents are intended exclusively for the owner and the campaign administrator or Dungeon Master (DM).

ONE PROJECT, TWO FORMATS

Versions.

01

Web application

Developed with Angular, Express, and MongoDB, it combines a web interface with a backend and a database to manage campaigns.

02

Android application

A mobile version that brings campaign management to the Android environment.
